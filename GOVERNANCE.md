# The Ryoku Project: governance

Ryoku is a founder-led, community-first open-source passion project. This document describes the project's current working model, not the bylaws of a corporation or nonprofit.

## Direction and participation

[neur0map](https://github.com/neur0map) is the project lead and final decision-maker for the Ryoku projects maintained here. Contributors help shape priorities through discussion, testing, documentation, design, and code. Maintainers may be given responsibility for specific repositories or subsystems; that responsibility should be recorded in the relevant repository.

Decisions consider usefulness, coherence with Ryoku's design, security, licensing, available knowledge, and the ability to maintain the result. Community participation does not guarantee that every proposed feature will be included. Explain substantial decisions and discuss large changes before implementation.

The project is maintained in spare time. There is no guaranteed release schedule, response time, or commercial support commitment. Project and platform-specific requirements belong in each repository.

## Access and stewardship

Use individual GitHub accounts, not shared logins. Grant the least access needed for a maintainer's responsibilities. Organization ownership is a high-trust operational role, not a reward for contribution volume or sponsorship.

As the maintainer group grows, establish a trusted second owner and document recovery and succession arrangements. Never publish recovery codes, signing keys, or private credentials in the public governance repository.

The officially recognized [Ryoku on NixOS](https://github.com/aethctl/Ryoku-on-NixOS) port retains its own repository ownership and maintainership. Recognition does not give this organization administrative control over that repository.

## Funding and independence

Funding does not buy automatic inclusion of a product or control over the roadmap. Evaluate proposed integrations on their merits for users and maintainers, and disclose material sponsorship arrangements if they arise.

Funding links must accurately identify who receives the money and what it supports. Do not imply that contributions are tax-deductible or that the project is a registered charity without the applicable legal basis.

## Licenses and assets

Each repository's license, upstream notices, and contributor copyrights remain authoritative. GitHub organization ownership is not ownership of every contribution's copyright. Repository transfers must preserve attribution and fork history.

If a fiscal sponsor or legal entity becomes useful later, document its responsibilities, governance, asset arrangements, and relationship to technical leadership before representing it as the project's steward. The GitHub organization alone does not establish nonprofit status.

## Conduct and concerns

Follow the affected project's code of conduct and security reporting policy. For private stewardship questions or concerns about project leadership, contact [neuro@ryoku.dev](mailto:neuro@ryoku.dev). This address currently reaches the project lead; it is not an independent ombudsperson or guaranteed-response service.
